# M1.1 financial calculation contract

[M1.1 issue #2](https://github.com/amberdemon/vestor/issues/2) · [M1 tracker](https://github.com/amberdemon/vestor/issues/1)

Rule version `uk-eng-ni-2026-27-v1`, effective **6 April 2026 through 5 April 2027 inclusive**, sources checked **6 November 2026**. Supports a full-year UK resident employee under England or Northern Ireland rules who qualifies for the standard Personal Allowance. A full year of residence can include part-year employment. Scotland, Wales, other years, split-year or cross-border cases, director-specific receipt rules and special allowances are outside this release. Never fall back to another ruleset.

## Dates and taxable amounts

Use ISO date-only values. Period endpoints are inclusive. Split a period at every change to annual rate, pension basis or taxable benefit rate. Intersect each resulting period with the selected tax year. A change on 1 November ends the prior rate on 31 October. Reject overlaps within one salary or benefit schedule. Explicitly identify uncovered employment days as unpaid or out of employment. Unexplained gaps are incomplete.

- **Salary and bonus.** Prefer actual dated payroll amounts with known taxable composition. For ordinary money earnings, use the earlier of payment (including payment on account) and entitlement to payment. For forecasts, use the expected taxable receipt date for a known pay amount. When only an annual salary rate and effective dates are known, estimate gross salary for each segment as annual rate × in-year covered days ÷ days in the selected tax year. Label this as a calendar-day estimate. It cannot resolve payroll timing differences across 5/6 April. Allocate bonuses once on their taxable receipt dates, with no proration or allocation to performance period. An expected payment date is a forecast assumption only if there is no known earlier entitlement. A confirmed payment with uncertain material entitlement timing is incomplete. [HMRC EIM42260](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42260)
- **Benefits.** Use confirmed payroll or P11D taxable GBP for the stated dates or tax year. Count a payrolled amount once. Require an explicit inclusion flag tied to the payroll record when it could already be in taxable pay. For forecasts, accept an annual **taxable value** only where the user confirms that daily apportionment is appropriate for the benefit. Apply the same day fraction as salary and show the rate, days and assumption. A premium alone leaves taxability or valuation unresolved. Keep employee payments as evidence and accept the supported taxable figure after applicable adjustments. Benefit-specific valuation, exemptions and optional remuneration calculations are outside M1. [HMRC medical benefit valuation example](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-and-benefits-medical-treatment/work-out-the-value)
- **RSUs.** Accept the gross taxable GBP employment amount on the taxable vest date for ordinary supported vest events. Require a user-entered taxable GBP figure for forecasts and prefer the confirmed payroll GBP amount and date for actuals. Record whether the amount is included in taxable payroll. An RSU or benefit already included remains visible as an included component. Preserve the payroll total and derive the salary component by removing any identified bonus, RSU and benefit inclusions once before reporting the breakdown. Incomplete component amounts or conflicting inclusion evidence block the total. Withholding and share sales do not enter the waterfall. Nonstandard awards and uncertain charge dates are outside M1. [HMRC employment-related securities guidance](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-related-securities/ersm20194)
- **Other income.** Accept UK taxable bank interest, taxable dividends and manually determined nonnegative net taxable UK trading or property profits. Enter taxable amounts before savings or dividend nil-rate bands. Explicitly exempt amounts such as ISA interest contribute zero and appear in the breakdown. Use the supplied taxable receipt date for interest/dividends or the stated assessment tax year for trading/property profits. Require the user to resolve ambiguous taxability or assessment dates. Foreign income, trust income, losses and other categories needing separate tax rules are outside M1. [HMRC ANI guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/adjusted-net-income)

## M1.4 salary and bonus domain

`calculate_employment` in `vestor_domain.employment` accepts salary periods, dated salary payments and dated bonus events for selected tax rules. A zero annual rate explicitly marks unpaid or out-of-employment dates. A missing rate or uncovered tax-year day leaves salary incomplete. Confirm bonus coverage explicitly, including a year with no bonuses. Pay frequency records evidence for a rate estimate. Supply dated payment amounts to model a specific pay schedule.

A dated salary payment links to each overlapping rate period it supersedes. The payment replaces the estimate only on the covered service days, even when its taxable receipt falls outside the selected year. A confirmed dated payment can also replace a dated forecast for the same service days. Forecasts for other days remain. A dated amount is allocated in full to its receipt date. The earlier-of-payment-and-entitlement rule applies to ordinary employee earnings under the verified 2026/27 rules. Payment includes payment on account. For actuals with uncertain entitlement timing, supply the payment date as evidence and flag the timing as uncertain. If the date of receipt is unknown, a source reference date is retained as evidence only. Material unresolved timing blocks the subtotal. [HMRC EIM42260, reviewed 6 November 2026](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42260)

Dated payroll preserves the reported amount and its confirmed composition. Included bonuses link to separate dated bonus records with matching amounts and pay basis, including the same reflected pension methods. A linked bonus payment or confirmed taxable receipt dated after the payroll payment leaves the result incomplete. An earlier bonus entitlement remains valid. Identified benefit and RSU components are deducted when isolating salary and retained as source evidence. M1.4 does not value those components. Exact salary and bonus subtotals are available where the contributing lines are all before payroll pension reductions. Pay reported after reductions remains on its stated basis. The employment calculator leaves pension reductions to the M1.5 domain. Known line amounts remain available when other evidence is incomplete.

## M1.5 pensions and Gift Aid domain

`calculate_pensions` in `vestor_domain.pensions` combines a resolved `EmploymentResult` with linked `PayrollReduction` and dated `Adjustment` records for the same verified rules and tax year. The caller confirms coverage or explicit absence of contributions and donations. The result retains source IDs, notes, dates, the employment lines and basis, exact eligible and applied amounts, the basic relief rate, and warnings. It exposes taxable salary and bonus, gross pension deductions for the net income step, and the separate relief-at-source and Gift Aid deductions for the ANI step.

- A payroll reduction links to one salary period, dated salary payment or bonus. A salary sacrifice rate or annual fixed amount is applied only to the intersection of the election's effective dates and remaining estimated service days. Changes use inclusive date ranges with no overlaps. The election must precede the first affected day. A dated sacrifice can use a fixed amount or percentage and must take effect before the taxable receipt date, including any earlier entitlement or payment on account. Date-only evidence cannot establish that a dated sacrifice took effect earlier on the same day.
- Net pay uses a dated payroll deduction amount or percentage. Full pay uses the remaining pay after salary sacrifice, and a stated eligible subset for net pay must already account for any sacrifice affecting it. When the net-pay deduction and taxable receipt fall in different tax years, the forecast adjustment is outside this domain's supported scope. Model net-pay forecasts with dated earnings and payroll deductions.
- A dated pay record explicitly before reductions has neither method reflected. A record after reductions can name the reflected methods or declare the reported amount fully after both. Linked reflected deductions record the observed amount and apply zero. When net pay alone is reflected and a sacrifice still applies, the known net-pay amount establishes the pre-sacrifice base, with the sacrifice capped by the remaining pay. A forecast reduction tied to replaced pay does not carry across to the actual.
- A personal payment record supplies its actual or expected effective date. An optional record date retains provider or receipt evidence separately. A confirmed provider gross relief-at-source amount may stand alone or accompany the net payment in the same record. Gift Aid and net-only relief at source use the 20% basic relief rate and exact gross-up. Ordinary employer contributions and already reflected payroll amounts do not supply deductions to either personal adjustment step.
- Qualification is declared evidence for the supported treatment. For sacrifice this includes a valid contractual change, employer scheme eligibility and minimum wage compliance. For Gift Aid this includes a declaration and sufficient tax. The domain does not verify individual eligibility or calculate annual allowance consequences. Unknown eligibility or dates on material records block the totals.

Rules applicable **6 April 2026 through 5 April 2027**, reviewed **6 November 2026**. [HMRC sacrifice timing](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42765) · [HMRC salary sacrifice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/salary-sacrifice-and-the-effects-on-paye) · [HMRC net pay](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044230) · [HMRC pension contribution dates](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm041000#deemed-date-of-contributions) · [HMRC ANI guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/adjusted-net-income) · [HMRC Gift Aid eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/donating-to-charity/gift-aid)

## M1.6 taxable benefit domain

`calculate_benefits` in `vestor_domain.benefits` accepts effective-dated records and the employment result for the same rules and tax year. Confirm that all benefits or their absence are represented. The result keeps the entered values, coverage, forecast day fractions, replacement links, employment evidence, taxable amounts and warnings.

- Forecasts use a separately entered **annual taxable value** after applicable adjustments, multiplied by included calendar days divided by calendar days in the selected UK tax year. Confirm that this planning convention suits the benefit. Endpoints are inclusive and each renewal starts on its new effective date. In F3, £2,400 × 209/365 plus £3,000 × 156/365 is £969,600/365, displayed as £2,656.44.
- An actual payroll or P11D value covers the stated inclusive dates and is taken in full when that coverage is within the tax year. Supply a confirmed in-year amount if the coverage straddles the boundary. Amounts wholly outside the year are excluded. An explicitly linked actual supersedes its same-benefit forecasts only on the confirmed overlapping days. Remaining forecast days keep their original rate. Other overlaps for one benefit are errors.
- A taxable period declares whether its value is included in entered payroll. Included benefits link to the active salary payments listing the same benefit period ID as a component, with the same evidence status and a matching combined amount. P11D evidence is separate from payroll. A component without matching benefit evidence or an ambiguous inclusion blocks the subtotal. Combine the full benefit subtotal with salary and bonus already isolated by the employment/pension domains. The payroll-included portion is also shown for reconciliation to the raw entered payroll.
- A declared exemption excludes the benefit with its reason. Unknown taxability or an unresolved exemption produces a warning and suppresses the subtotal. Annual premiums, payer and employee contributions are evidence. The domain uses the entered taxable amount after applicable adjustments. Product-specific statutory valuation, optional remuneration and taxability decisions require external confirmation.

Rules applicable **6 April 2026 through 5 April 2027**, reviewed **6 November 2026**. [HMRC medical benefit valuation](https://www.gov.uk/expenses-and-benefits-medical-treatment/work-out-the-value) · [HMRC general valuation and employee payments](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim21102) · [HMRC voluntary payrolling and P11D reporting](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/paying-your-employees-expenses-and-benefits-through-your-payroll#how-voluntary-payrolling-works)

## Payroll reductions and ANI waterfall

1. Start with taxable salary and taxable bonus after any eligible payroll reductions. For an input explicitly before reductions, subtract valid salary sacrifice on its stated eligible base once, then deduct the net-pay employee contribution actually applied (or forecast to apply) on the corresponding payroll. Use a fixed GBP amount for a dated payment or a percentage of the stated eligible pre-sacrifice salary or bonus. An annual fixed salary sacrifice uses the salary day fraction. A forecast net-pay amount uses a dated fixed payroll deduction or a percentage on its stated eligible salary or bonus base, after any salary sacrifice affecting that base. For actual taxable pay already after a reduction, report the contribution as evidence and apply no further reduction. Require the basis to be explicit for each method. No reduction can exceed the remaining eligible pay. Require confirmation of a valid prospective contractual sacrifice, including employer eligibility and minimum wage constraints. Ordinary employer pension funding contributes zero to both taxable pay and ANI deductions. [HMRC salary sacrifice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/salary-sacrifice-and-the-effects-on-paye) · [HMRC net pay](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044230)
2. Add taxable benefits and GBP RSU income exactly once to obtain employment income. Add supported other taxable income before Personal Allowance. Subtract any separately confirmed qualifying pension contribution paid gross without prior relief, pound for pound, to obtain HMRC net income. M1 does not accept generic deduction entries or compute loss relief or trade union/police add-backs.
3. Subtract qualifying cash Gift Aid in the year of donation and qualifying relief-at-source pension in the tax year of payment. Each gross deduction is net payment ÷ (1 − 0.20), or net × 1.25. If a provider confirms a gross relief-at-source amount, use that amount with its evidence once. Never add another deduction for any higher-rate relief. Require confirmation of qualification, including sufficient tax for Gift Aid and pension relief eligibility. Exclude Gift Aid carry-back, Payroll Giving, and pension eligibility/allowance calculations. Use the confirmed effective contribution date under HMRC payment-method rules for personal contributions, including relief at source paid through payroll. The provider's later tax-relief credit date does not move the contribution. Net pay uses the payroll deduction date. Forecasts use the expected effective date. If the actual effective date cannot be confirmed, mark it incomplete. [HMRC ANI steps](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/adjusted-net-income) · [HMRC pension contribution dates](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm041000#deemed-date-of-contributions) · [HMRC Gift Aid eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/donating-to-charity/gift-aid)
4. Report ANI after those adjustments. Keep the pre-adjustment totals, net income and each adjustment visible. For an entered target, report signed headroom as target minus ANI. The statutory threshold is separate from the target. An absent optional target suppresses headroom only.
5. Compute the standard Personal Allowance as `ceil(max(0, 12570 - max(0, ANI - 100000) / 2))`, using ANI before display rounding. The whole-pound ceiling applies to the **remaining allowance**. [HMRC 2026/27 rates and allowances](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-income-tax/income-tax-rates-and-allowances-current-and-past) · [Income Tax Act 2007, section 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/35)

## Precision, evidence and result states

- Accept finite nonnegative GBP decimal strings with up to two fractional digits and percentage strings from 0 to 100 with up to four fractional digits. Zero is a valid explicit amount. Reject malformed values, invalid dates, currency mismatches, repeated event identifiers, reversed periods, inconsistent payroll composition and reductions exceeding their pay base. A computed result such as headroom can be negative.
- Parse inputs as exact decimals. Keep exact rational values for intermediate arithmetic, including nonterminating day fractions, to avoid rounding errors at threshold boundaries. Round GBP displays once to pennies using half-up (ties away from zero), except for the statutory allowance ceiling. Calculate totals from unrounded values. Independently rounded lines can differ from the displayed total. Disclose that difference when present.
- Give events and covered payroll/benefit periods stable identities and retain dates, source notes, basis and evidence. An explicitly linked actual replaces the matching forecast before year filtering, even if the corrected date moves the event to another year. For recurring amounts, replace only the confirmed coverage and retain forecasts for uncovered days. A confirmed full-year benefit amount replaces the full-year forecast for that benefit. Do not infer a match from date or amount. Flag ambiguous overlap or reconciliation and withhold the total until resolved.
- Keep source status (`actual`, `forecast` or `unknown`) separate from whether a value is known. For valid inputs, result status is `confirmed` when all relevant inputs are actual and the user confirms coverage, `estimated` when a supported forecast or estimation method contributes, `incomplete` when a material input or coverage is unknown, and `unsupported` when the year, jurisdiction or declared tax treatment is outside scope. Unsupported takes precedence over incomplete. Do not return numeric ANI, allowance or headroom for incomplete or unsupported results. Known lines may still be shown as partial evidence. Require explicit zero, exemption or absence for relevant input categories before treating them as contributing zero. Invalid inputs produce field errors.
- Return status, dated included/excluded/replaced lines, source links or notes, assumptions, warnings and ruleset version. Keep taxable income, withholding, gains and cash separate. No tax bill, take-home pay, HICBC, CGT, FX conversion, pension optimisation, or persistence is covered here.

Day weighting and presentation rounding are planning conventions. Daily rates can differ from employer payroll or benefit valuation. The Personal Allowance ceiling and ANI adjustments above follow the cited rules. Any new rule version requires a dated source review and rerun of the boundary fixtures.
