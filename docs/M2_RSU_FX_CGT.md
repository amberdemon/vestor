# M2 financial contract — RSUs, FX and Capital Gains Tax

[M2 tracker #24](https://github.com/amberdemon/vestor/issues/24) · [Contract issue #25](https://github.com/amberdemon/vestor/issues/25) · [M1 financial contract](M1_FINANCIAL_CONTRACT.md) · [M1 release evidence](M1_RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md)

This contract defines planned M2 behaviour. The annual rules version is `uk-eng-ni-cgt-2026-27-v1`, effective 6 April 2026 through 5 April 2027 inclusive. The existing M1 ANI rules keep their own version. Primary sources were checked on 14 November 2026.

## Supported awards, dates and evidence

M2 covers a UK-resident individual in the existing M1 England/Northern Ireland scope, holding ordinary unrestricted listed shares personally in a taxable account. One share identity is issuer, class, beneficial owner and capacity. Include matching shares across all brokers in that capacity. Ticker text alone does not establish identity. The supported RSU forecast assumes a right to receive ordinary shares with no exercise consideration or additional components, a confirmed award treatment, and simultaneous vest, acquisition and UK employment charge. Preserve distinct dates even when they coincide. Cash alternatives, dividend equivalents, net share withholding, restricted or convertible shares, elections, separate option schemes, corporate actions, wrappers, joint or other capacities, short sales and cross-border or split-year cases are outside scope. Record unresolved evidence and block the affected result.

| Evidence | Employment-income action | CGT acquisition action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed gross taxable payroll GBP and confirmed UK charge date | Use payroll GBP on the charge date. Retain original FMV, FX estimate and signed payroll minus model difference if available. | Require independent acquisition basis evidence. A payroll number alone does not establish basis. |
| Supported clean award and dated FMV with dated FX, payroll figure pending | Use gross shares times FMV times charge-date FX as an estimate. An actual vest can still have estimated valuation. | Use a separately supported basis or leave the basis unresolved. |
| Unrestricted employment acquisition where the market-value rule applies | Resolve income using the preceding rows. | Use acquisition-date GBP market value, plus any separately established qualifying acquisition expense. Do not add the same taxed value again. |
| Supported Chapter 5 RSU acquisition with verified nil exercise price, no other consideration and confirmed qualifying section 476 income component | Resolve income using the preceding rows. | Section 144ZA displaces market value where applicable. Use actual consideration plus the independently evidenced qualifying section 119A addition. A source can support both figures when it explicitly confirms both treatments. For a clean forecast with established treatment, the modelled acquisition-date market value can estimate the nil-price section 476 amount if it represents the full qualifying amount. |
| Missing date, unclear treatment, deferred acquisition/charge, restrictions or unexplained adjustment | Block material unresolved income unless the M1 manual route independently supports the case. | Block the unsupported or incomplete basis path. Preserve original evidence and reason. |

The ordinary Chapter 5 award and market-value rules depend on the facts. Section 119A additions cannot be inferred from net payroll, employer NI recovery or broker display basis. [HMRC ERSM20192](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-related-securities/ersm20192), [ERSM20194 example 1](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-related-securities/ersm20194), [ERSM180020](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-related-securities/ersm180020), [CG56321](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56321), [CG56321A](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56321a), [CG56328](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56328), [CG56384](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg56384).

For an unconditional arm’s-length sale the trade contract date controls disposal, matching, GBP proceeds and tax year. Record settlement and transfer dates separately. Convert USD acquisition cost and disposal proceeds individually on their relevant dates. Each rate has a stable identity, basis date, positive GBP-per-USD value, source and confirmed/estimated status. Use a documented, consistently applied reasonable rate. An execution or wire rate remains cash evidence unless independently justified for the relevant tax purpose. Missing rate evidence blocks the dependent calculation. No nearest-day, statutory-month-average or last-rate fallback. A derived rate for a nontrading day requires explicit date and method provenance. [HMRC CG14250 and TCGA s28](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg14250), [HMRC CG78310](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg78310).

Record gross trade consideration and each fee once. Document disposal expenses as allowable only on evidence of an eligible expense wholly and exclusively incurred for that disposal, such as an eligible dealing commission. Use the disposal-date GBP valuation for supported trade-date fees. Record a separately dated or uncertain tax expense as unresolved until its tax treatment and GBP valuation are established. A sale fee applies even if withheld from settlement. A wire charge, remittance spread, payroll withholding or repayment has no automatic CGT deduction. Acquisition expenses already included in confirmed basis are never added again. [HMRC CG15250 and TCGA s38](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg15250).

## Share matching and conservation

1. Validate original evidence and resolve explicit replacements before matching. Start from complete history or an exact, evidenced, dated opening Section 104 quantity and allowable GBP cost. The opening state must reconcile any residual effect of pre-opening dispositions and their lookahead. Require confirmed coverage across all same-identity transactions through 30 days after each sale used in a confirmed result. Physical holdings and the tax pool may differ while later acquisitions are reserved. Missing history is incomplete. A physical oversale or short sale is unsupported.
2. Group same-identity acquisitions and disposals separately per day. Combine quantities, acquisition bases, proceeds and eligible fees exactly. Reserve same-day acquisition units against the aggregate disposal first on every day, including later days. Cost of any slice of a daily acquisition is quantity times the day’s weighted-average acquisition cost.
3. For each residual disposal day in ascending order, consume eligible unused residual acquisitions in ascending date order, 1 through 30 calendar days later inclusive. The seller must be UK resident at the later acquisition. Known nonresidence is outside supported scope. Day 31 cannot consume under this rule. A later acquisition is never consumed twice. Cross-year acquisition dates can change a prior-year disposal basis, never the disposal year.
4. Process pool movements in date order. Only acquisition residue after both reservations enters the pool. Debit a residual sale by pool cost times quantity sold from pool divided by pool quantity immediately before debit. Never manufacture negative pool quantities or use the exceptional later-acquisition rule under section 105(2) in M2. If this rule might be required or history cannot support a full match, suppress the affected gain and annual estimate. Keep source-linked known segments.
5. Compute each day’s statutory gain as gross GBP proceeds minus eligible sale costs minus total matched basis. For an original sale row, show its own proceeds and costs with its share of the daily matched basis by quantity. This display attribution sums exactly to the tax day total. Show original sales and aggregate tax day separately. For each identity assert total acquisition quantity and cost, including opening pool, equal matched plus remaining quantities and costs. Keep actual and forecast scenarios separate or explicitly labelled. Forecast lookahead makes affected matches provisional. A newly confirmed acquisition or replacement triggers recomputation of all affected disposals and pool movements, including earlier tax years.

Authority is [TCGA 1992 ss104–106A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/part/IV/chapter/I/crossheading/share-pooling-identification-of-securities-and-indexation), [HMRC CG51555](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51555), [CG51560](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51560), [CG51575](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg51575) and [HS284 (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/shares-and-capital-gains-tax-hs284-self-assessment-helpsheet/hs284-shares-and-capital-gains-tax-2026). HS284 (2026) is for 2025/26 and corroborates the share rules. The 2026/27 rates come from the sources below.

## Qualified annual CGT estimate

Recognise gains and losses by disposal date. Require explicit full-year completeness or absence of other gains and allowable losses, available claimed brought-forward losses, and eligibility for the £3,000 annual exempt amount. Support other externally resolved gains/losses only if they can be aggregated under the same ordinary rules and rates without double counting. Special rates/reliefs, restricted losses, FIG/OWR claims, prior-year tax calculation and other years’ annual estimates are outside this release.

For 2026/27, add positive gains and subtract allowable current-year losses. Report any excess current-year loss separately. For positive net gain, use brought-forward losses up to the smaller of the known balance and net gain above £3,000. Exempt up to £3,000 of what remains and preserve unused brought-forward losses. Apply 18% to the taxable gain within the externally evidenced unused basic-rate band and 24% to the rest. The unextended band is £37,700. Evidence may be an externally established unused band, or externally established taxable income after applicable allowances and confirmed applicable extension evidence. Eligible gross relief-at-source pension and Gift Aid can extend the band. Require known extensions or explicit absence. ANI, gross salary and take-home pay are different quantities and cannot substitute. Where CGT band, allowance eligibility, annual gains/losses or ledger history is unknown, retain known disposal gains with warnings and suppress the estimate. Even with confirmed inputs this is a qualified estimate.

Rates and allowance checked against [2026/27 Annex A](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-overview-of-tax-legislation-and-rates-ootlar/annex-a-rates-and-allowances), [HMRC current CGT rates](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/capital-gains-tax-rates-and-allowances) and [TCGA ss1H–1J](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/part/1/chapter/1/crossheading/rates-of-cgt/2026-04-06?view=plain). See [HMRC losses](https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/losses) and [CG21204 for band mechanics](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg21204). CG21204’s historical rate examples do not supply 2026/27 rates.

## ANI bridge, cash and local interfaces

- Retain original stable IDs, actual/forecast status and provenance. Permit one explicit actual replacement per compatible forecast for the same event, applied before tax-year filtering. Preserve old evidence. Equality in dates or amounts never proves identity. An M2 vest feeds one M1 RSU component through explicit links to the canonical manual vest or payroll-inclusion record. Confirm compatible charge dates and gross taxable GBP evidence, and flag ambiguous or conflicting links. Do not add a vest already included in a payroll amount. Manual M1 evidence can still stand alone. Incomplete CGT or cash evidence does not independently invalidate confirmed ANI.
- Record sell-to-cover as an actual or forecast trade disposal linked to its vest. Record remittances, tax/NI liabilities, amounts withheld, repayments and outstanding status as separate explicit records. A sale is not evidence that the tax was remitted. Record gross and net broker proceeds, cash balances, transfers, execution FX and fees, and received GBP as separate dated evidence. Reconcile only explicitly linked amounts with matching basis and coverage. Outstanding amounts must be evidenced. Neither withholding nor cash settlements change gross ANI or matched basis. Estimated CGT does not reduce observed cash.
- Keep the existing M1 calculation route compatible. Add a separate versioned M2 request/response using the existing M1 domain and one share ledger. Accept strict local JSON with ISO dates, decimal strings, explicit zero/none/unknown evidence, validated identities and unknown-key rejection. Return exact rational numerator/denominator strings and half-up penny displays. Never round intermediate money, FX, quantities or the pool. Disclose rounding differences between displayed rows and totals.
- Return independently scoped ANI, ledger, annual CGT and cash statuses and warnings with evidence IDs, dates, rules versions, assumptions, exact match segments and pool movements. Expose unknown, incomplete and unsupported cases at the affected result. The UI presents these separate amounts and keeps financial math in Python. Inputs are transient, loopback-only and unsaved. Do not log source data or call external services during core use. Full income tax/NI, spendable cash, advice, tax filing and persistence are outside scope.

## Decisions for subsequent issues

Reserve all same-day quantities before allocating later-day matches. Use exact weighted allocation within daily groups and keep input rows for audit. Use the strict versioned M2 boundary with the existing M1 reconciliation as the authority for ANI. Use an externally evidenced basic-rate band or its documented inputs until a separately verified income-tax model exists. These decisions avoid broker-specific assumptions and provide deterministic acceptance fixtures.

Unresolved variations require a new financial decision before support: differing vest, acquisition and tax-charge timing, broker net share releases, special share scheme elections, complex eligibility and multi-rate CGT, unsupported historical pool changes, and tax eligibility of currency-related expenses beyond directly evidenced dealing costs. The current path reports warning/status instead of guessing their treatment.
