**Status: normative implementation contract, not shipped functionality.** M1/M2 remain the implemented product. This document governs the whole M3 milestone; later issue PRs implement its parts. Contract approval requires an independent different-model financial review of the exact final head. Milestone acceptance also requires its final integration/verification issue, local UI, canonical checks and recorded limitations.

Rules researched **26 November 2026**, effective **6 April 2026 through 5 April 2027 inclusive**. Proposed additional version: `uk-eng-ni-m3-2026-27-v1`; retain M1 `uk-eng-ni-2026-27-v1` and M2 `uk-eng-ni-cgt-2026-27-v1` provenance independently. These are pinned versions, not an automatic latest-rules selector. Recheck sources before release; never apply announced or enacted 2027/28 rules to 2026/27.

## 1. Supported outcome and boundaries

M3 must compare immutable future compensation scenarios, estimate supported annual income tax and employee Class 1 NI, find the minimum eligible extra gross pension funding for a target ANI plus buffer, and compare pension invested, after-tax cash foregone and remaining tax provision separately. It supports bonus-only, monthly payroll and personal relief-at-source (RAS) alternatives, including a feasible combination/fallback when one route is insufficient.

Support full-year UK resident employees in England/Northern Ireland, standard Personal Allowance, ordinary employment/bonus/benefits, M2-qualified listed-share RSUs, UK savings interest and ordinary dividends. Preserve other M1-supported income in ANI. Nonnegative manually established UK trading/property profits may enter annual non-savings income only with confirmation that their taxable totals and deductions are settled; Class 2/4 liabilities and property finance-cost relief are outside the employee NI engine. A material such liability/relief without externally confirmed provision blocks complete spendable cash, rather than treating it as zero.

Unsupported tax cases include Scotland/Wales, other tax years, split-year/foreign relief/remittance/FIG cases, Marriage/Blind Person's Allowance, losses/reliefs beyond supported inputs, student loans, HICBC, pension annual-allowance charges and nonstandard equity. Do not erase known ANI/gain/evidence when a separate tax/cash result is blocked. Require explicit absence or evidence for material liabilities; unknown is never zero. Complex pension cases can use externally confirmed eligibility limits as below; M3 does not calculate taper/carry-forward/MPAA/defined-benefit growth.

Annual income-tax liability is a planning estimate, not PAYE tax-code simulation, payroll software or a tax-return computation. Cash timing must use actual/forecast payroll deductions separately. No persistence/export, remote prices/FX, accounts or bank/broker feeds; M4 retains that scope.

## 2. Evidence, dates and immutable scenarios

A scenario identifies its baseline revision, tax year, jurisdiction, as-of date, rules versions, named overrides and source IDs. Preserve the entire source baseline. Each override targets a specific **forecast** record/field, retains its original value and gives a date, assumption/status and explanation. Permit dated vest price/FX, sale price/FX/timing/quantity, bonus, taxable benefit renewal, interest/dividends, pension percentage/amount/base, target and buffer changes. Percentages require an explicit eligible compensation base; never silently apply a base-salary percentage to bonus or RSUs.

Reject overrides of actuals, missing/ambiguous IDs, incompatible dates/purposes, duplicate conflicting overrides and attempts to revise an already effective sacrifice election retrospectively. Actual payroll GBP remains authoritative for taxable income; a forecast quote cannot replace it. Corrections use the existing explicit evidence replacement/reconciliation path, retaining originals. Scenarios are transient local values until M4; immutability does not imply durable storage.

Re-evaluate active forecasts through M1/M2. A sale-date change can change tax year, same-day and 30-day matches, pool, gains, FX and receipt timing; require matching coverage for the new window. Vest price changes employment income and acquisition basis only through their independently qualified valuations. Sale proceeds/gain never become ANI. No predicted market return is represented as fact.

Keep payment, entitlement/taxable receipt, vest/acquisition, disposal trade, FX execution, bank receipt, election and pension-payment dates distinct. A contribution paid on 6 April cannot repair preceding-year ANI. Employer/provider cutoffs may precede 5 April and are user-evidenced, not guessed.

## 3. Annual income tax

Classify income into non-savings (N), savings (S) and dividends (D). Reuse M1 net-income/ANI and pension reconciliation; ANI itself is **not** taxable income. Deduct net-pay contributions only where not already in taxable pay. RAS does not subtract its gross amount from N/S/D: it reduces ANI and extends the basic and higher rate limits. Qualifying gross Gift Aid likewise adjusts ANI and extends limits; do not award pension funding for it. Gross pension relief already given elsewhere must not be duplicated.

For 2026/27 standard PA is £12,570. Above ANI £100,000 it is reduced by half the excess, floored at zero, then rounded **up to whole pounds**, using exact unrounded ANI. [ITA 2007 s35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/35). Allocate available PA across income components to minimise liability under [ITA s25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/25), respecting any restriction on a deduction. Document the chosen allocation and compare exact candidate liabilities at relevant allowance/band breakpoints. Do not fix an employment-first allocation if it is less beneficial. The revised page includes s25(3A), but its commencement is **2027/28 onwards**, not this ruleset.

Stack income after deductions as N then S then D under [ITA s16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/16). Basic taxable-income limit £37,700, higher limit £125,140; extend both by qualifying gross RAS/Gift Aid. N/S rates are 20%/40%/45%; D rates 10.75%/35.75%/39.35%. [2026/27 income-tax allowances](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-income-tax/income-tax-rates-and-allowances-current-and-past), [Finance Act 2026, income-tax provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/11/part/1/crossheading/income-tax-charge-rates-and-allowances/2026-08-17).

Savings starting-rate capacity is max(0, £5,000 minus taxable N); apply it first to eligible S. Personal Savings Allowance (PSA) is £1,000 for basic-rate, £500 for higher-rate, zero for additional-rate taxpayers, determined using all taxable income and extended limits, including nil-rate income. Apply remaining PSA to S after starting-rate savings. Dividend allowance is £500. These are **nil-rate slices**, still consuming bands and included in ANI; they are not additional PA deductions. Apply nil-rate slices from the lowest relevant part of that income, retaining their band occupancy. ISA/exempt income is excluded only with explicit exemption evidence. [HMRC savings allowances](https://www.gov.uk/apply-tax-free-interest-on-savings/how-much-is-tax-free), [HMRC dividends](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-dividends).

Return gross categories, net-income deductions, ANI deductions, PA calculation/allocation, extended limits, starting-rate/PSA/dividend slices, each rate allocation, exact total and displayed total. Effective tax = supported income-tax liability / gross taxable income before PA (zero denominator returns no rate). Marginal comparison is the recomputed scenario delta divided by its specified compensation delta, with both denominators named; never apply a single 40%/60% shortcut across boundaries.

## 4. Employee Class 1 NI

Support one ordinary non-director employment, category A, standard weekly or monthly earnings periods, exact-percentage calculation, with fully dated NI-able earnings and coverage. Annual salary calendar-day forecasts alone cannot establish period NI: require/derive an explicitly labelled future pay schedule and reconcile it to the annual employment forecast. Do not divide annual thresholds by 12/52. Unsupported categories, directors, deferment, multiple aggregated jobs, irregular/overlapping periods, corrections/refunds and post-employment special cases need separate rule work or externally evidenced deductions; suppress modelled NI for those cases.

Monthly primary threshold £1,048 and upper earnings limit £4,189; weekly £242 and £967. Employee NI is 8% between the thresholds plus 2% above the upper limit, zero below primary threshold. Sum salary, bonus and confirmed NI-able RSU earnings **within the same period before thresholds**. Sum individually rounded period contributions for the annual amount. [HMRC 2026/27 employer thresholds](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027), [employee rates](https://www.gov.uk/national-insurance/how-much-you-pay).

Salary sacrifice reduces NI-able cash earnings where valid. Net pay and RAS do not. Ordinary taxable medical benefits generally carry employer Class 1A, not employee Class 1; require explicit classification for other benefits. For supported listed RSUs require confirmed readily-convertible-asset treatment, employee NI-able GBP amount and earnings-period allocation; no inference from taxable vest value alone. Include them even if no shares are sold, and never charge employee NI again on disposal. Employer NI transferred by election/agreement is unsupported without separately verified treatment; never subtract it automatically from employee taxable income. [NIM06835](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim06835), [ERS employer guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-employment-related-securities-work-if-youre-an-employer).

Round the combined employee contribution per period to nearest penny, with a half-penny or less disregarded (exact half-penny down); do not round separate 8% and 2% slices first. Keep exact pre-round amount and rounding difference. [SSCR 2001 reg12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1004/regulation/12), corroborated by the [2026/27 CWG2 exact-percentage instructions, section 2.7.5](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cwg2-further-guide-to-paye-and-national-insurance-contributions/2026-to-2027-employer-further-guide-to-paye-and-national-insurance-contributions#exact-percentage-method-used-to-work-out-national-insurance-contributions). Source conflict checked on 26 November: [NIM11002](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/national-insurance-manual/nim11002) paraphrases this as less than £0.005, which could imply half-up. This contract follows the regulation and current-year CWG2 explicit exact-half-penny-down instruction; use a separate NI rounding operation, not the existing general GBP half-up display helper. Employer NI savings are not employee cash or pension funding unless the employer explicitly commits a dated amount to the pension; include any such addition in capacity usage.

## 5. Pension feasibility and optimisation

Salary/bonus sacrifice needs an effective contractual change before taxable receipt/entitlement, an eligible unsacrificed amount, employer election cutoff and confirmation of minimum-wage compliance for every affected pay reference period. Use an employer-confirmed maximum reducible amount or verified minimum-wage/pay/hours evidence; a high annual salary is not proof. No retrospective sacrifice of actual pay or vested awards. [EIM42765](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/employment-income-manual/eim42765), [salary sacrifice guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/salary-sacrifice-and-the-effects-on-paye).

Net-pay funding is a gross employee deduction from taxable pay, without employee NI reduction. RAS gross is net payment / 0.8; basic-rate relief is pension funding, not received cash. Additional relief and restored PA affect annual liability; a future refund cannot be counted as cash already received. [PTM044230](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044230), [FA2004 s192](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12/section/192).

Require registered scheme, relevant UK individual/age eligibility, paid-in-year timing and externally confirmed remaining member tax-relief capacity, taking existing member contributions and relevant UK earnings into account. A qualifying individual under 75 may have £3,600 gross RAS relief capacity even with low/no earnings; this is not an annual allowance. Salary sacrifice is employer funding, not a member contribution subject to the member earnings cap. Do not count interest/dividends as relevant UK earnings. [PTM044100](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044100), [pension relief](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-your-private-pension/pension-tax-relief).

Separately require externally confirmed **remaining pension input capacity before an annual-allowance charge**, scope/year/as-of date and included existing/expected funding. Record gross member, employer and salary-sacrifice funding once. Standard annual allowance is £60,000; taper, MPAA, carry-forward and defined-benefit growth may alter capacity. Their calculation is unsupported; a dated externally confirmed cap is allowed, but Vestor must not claim to have established it. Unknown or exceeded capacity blocks an executable recommendation and qualified cash comparison, while the mathematical ANI gap remains visible. A change to earnings/funding that could invalidate confirmation requires reconfirmation. [HMRC annual allowance](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-your-private-pension/annual-allowance).

For target T, nonnegative buffer B, effective target is T−B. From complete baseline ANI A, mathematical gross reduction needed is **max(0, A−T+B)**. This formula applies only to eligible pound-for-pound ANI reductions. Do not round down to pennies. Find the smallest executable contribution (or payment schedule) achieving exact ANI ≤ T−B, respecting contribution increments, percentage steps, remaining pay, method caps, deadlines, minimum wage, earnings-relief limits and shared pension-input cap. Recalculate ANI after the proposed funding. RAS net penny increments imply theoretical gross increments of £0.0125 before provider rounding. Require the scheme’s evidenced gross-up/relief rounding and accepted contribution precision; solve on the actual permitted payment grid using the gross qualifying contribution that will be credited, not a rounded gross display. If that treatment is unknown, retain the mathematical gap but block the executable RAS minimum. Extra employer top-ups consume capacity but do not give extra ANI reduction.

Show bonus-only, monthly-only and personal-RAS routes independently, then a documented bonus/monthly/RAS combined alternative if necessary. Allocate monthly amounts to remaining eligible periods, with an exact residual in the final eligible period where permitted. A missed employer cutoff can invalidate bonus/monthly paths while leaving RAS possible before the provider cutoff. Existing payroll elections are baseline funding, never additional reductions. Compare optional net-pay changes only where the scheme permits them. Do not rank routes by gross gap alone: use recomputed annual tax and period NI, cash dates and liquidity. If unreachable, show maximum eligible reduction, residual target gap and each binding constraint; do not label a capped amount “minimum to reach target.”

## 6. Cash, liabilities and CGT interaction

Two views are required: dated liquid cash available within a chosen horizon, and annual economic after-tax comparison. Starting liquidity is explicit. Gross RSU vest value/retained shares/pension funding are not liquid cash. Cash includes evidenced payroll receipts and broker/GBP transfer receipts, not both a transfer and its underlying trade proceeds. Consolidated account transfers cancel; unconverted USD is shown separately unless an explicit conversion forecast exists. Show fees once, including fees already netted from receipts.

For each tax/liability identity, reconcile estimated annual liability with confirmed/forecast paid amounts and outstanding amounts. Provision is the positive outstanding balance; overpayment is separately a potential refund, not automatically spendable. A reported actual outstanding liability is evidence to reconcile against the model, not another additive bill. Withholding/remittance already reducing a payroll/broker receipt is credited against liability, not deducted from that receipt again. A later payment reduces both liquid cash and outstanding provision, preventing a second economic charge. Dated unpaid pension transfers reduce available cash; salary sacrifice already reduces payroll cash and is not deducted again. Unknown material fees, settlement, withholding or liabilities block qualified spendable cash.

Available-to-spend = liquid cash in scope − outstanding tax/other required provisions − committed unpaid cash outflows, with all terms dated and non-overlapping. Retain negative balances/shortfalls; never clamp them to zero. Do not add anticipated refunds to the immediate view before receipt; show their possible annual economic effect separately. Provision may relate to income/gain accrued before cash receipt, so the view must expose liquidity risk.

After-tax cash foregone = baseline annual economic cash − candidate annual economic cash over identical income/asset/horizon assumptions. Report pension funding increase separately, including RAS relief and confirmed employer additions. For RAS use the **net** cash payment and actual annual personal tax-liability change; adding the provider's basic-rate top-up to personal tax savings would double-count relief.

Recompute the available CGT basic band from the same supported income-tax calculation: max(0, extended basic limit − total taxable income after PA), including savings/dividends taxed at nil rates. Pass dated, versioned, scenario-derived evidence into the existing qualified M2 annual CGT engine, retaining M2's other-gains/losses/coverage gates. Never use ANI as that band, or carry the baseline band into a changed scenario. External band evidence remains usable only if confirmed compatible with the scenario; conflicts block the qualified annual CGT estimate. [CG21204](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/capital-gains-manual/cg21204) explains extension mechanics but carries an outdated rate warning: use M2's verified 2026/27 18%/24%, not its historical rates. CGT gains and cash fees never alter ANI.

## 7. Exact arithmetic and architecture

Reuse immutable M1/M2 evidence, `calculate_ani`, pensions/employment/benefits/other-income, vest/FX/disposal/share matching, annual CGT and settlement modules. Add pure scenario application, supported annual income-tax, period employee-NI, eligibility/optimisation and cash composition functions at their responsible seams. Do not reproduce M1 deductions or the M2 matcher inside an optimiser. A thin versioned calculation API and React forms/results consume these domain functions; no financial arithmetic in JavaScript, remote service, database or new dependency is required.

Money, quantities, percentages and rates remain decimal strings/exact fractions; no binary floats. Keep rational precision through tax bands, FX and comparisons. PA ceiling and period NI rounding above are statutory. Other annual income-tax/CGT and cash displays use half-up pennies only at output; retain exact totals and displayed-row discrepancy. This annual planning convention is not Self Assessment whole-pound input rounding or PAYE tables. Executable cash payments respect their supported penny/increment grid. Reject negative buffer, invalid dates/rates, incompatible currency, conflicting coverage and unlinked duplicates.

Each result has independent status (`confirmed`, `estimated`, `incomplete`, `unsupported`), exact/display values where valid, warnings, source IDs, dates, assumptions, selected rules and verification date. All future forecasts and recommendations are estimates even when their arithmetic is exact. A valid ANI gap can coexist with blocked eligibility, NI, CGT or spendable cash. UI must expose that distinction and clear stale calculations when inputs change.
